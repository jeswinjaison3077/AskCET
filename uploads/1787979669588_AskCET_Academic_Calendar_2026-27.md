## 2. Academic Year Overview

The B.Tech academic year at CET is structured (for demo purposes) into two semesters:

- **Odd Semester (Sample/Demo):** Runs approximately from **10 August 2026 to 04 January 2027**, covering Semesters 1, 3, 5, and 7 for the respective batches.
- **Even Semester (Sample/Demo):** Runs approximately from **05 January 2027 to 15 June 2027**, covering Semesters 2, 4, 6, and 8 for the respective batches.

Because different batches (First Year, Second Year, Third Year, Final Year) are enrolled in different semesters during the same academic year, this calendar lists **eight semester sections** — one for each semester number — all falling within Academic Year 2026–27.

**Major academic milestones (Sample/Demo):**

- Odd semester class commencement: 10 August 2026 (continuing batches) / 24 August 2026 (First Year)
- Odd semester end-examinations: December 2026
- Semester break (odd → even): Mid/late December 2026 to early January 2027
- Even semester class commencement: 05 January 2027
- Even semester end-examinations: April–May 2027
- Semester break (even → next odd, AY 2027–28): May–June 2027

All dates above are **Sample/Demo Dates** and are clearly distinguished from any real official CET/KTU announcement, none of which were provided as a source for this document.

## 10. Department/Semester Variations

Academic dates in this demo calendar may vary across:

- **Different programs:** This document covers the B.Tech program only. M.Tech, MCA, MBA, B.Arch, and PhD calendars are not included and would follow separate schedules.
- **Different semesters:** Odd-semester (1, 3, 5, 7) and even-semester (2, 4, 6, 8) dates differ, as shown in Section 3.
- **First-year students (Semester 1):** Follow a distinct commencement schedule tied to admission/reporting dates, generally starting later than continuing batches.
- **Final-year students (Semesters 7–8):** Include additional project and, where applicable, internship-related milestones not present in earlier semesters.
- **Internship/project semesters:** Semesters 5 through 8 include project-related milestones (mini project, main project) not applicable to Semesters 1–4.
- **Special academic schedules:** Department-specific lab schedules, elective tracks, or additional department notices are not covered in this institution-wide demo calendar and would need to be sourced separately.

## 11. Calendar Change Policy

Academic dates published in an academic calendar may be modified through subsequent official notices issued by the College or University.

- **Who may issue changes (if verified):** Typically the Controller of Examinations, the Academic/Examination Cell, or the Principal's office at the institutional level, and the University Examination Controller at the KTU level. No specific issuing authority is confirmed for this demo document, as no official source was provided.
- **How students are informed:** Normally through official college notice boards, the official college/university website, or official circulars. This demo document does not reference any real notice or URL.
- **Importance of checking the latest official notice:** Students should always verify dates against the most recent official communication rather than relying solely on a previously published academic calendar.

**Important statement:** When a newer official notice changes a date in the academic calendar, the latest applicable official notice should be treated as the current information.
