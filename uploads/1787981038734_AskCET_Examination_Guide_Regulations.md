## 1. EXAMINATION SYSTEM OVERVIEW

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 (R6), KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 (R7)*

KTU B.Tech follows a **semester system** with two regular semesters per academic year:
- **Odd Semester:** 1 July – 31 December
- **Even Semester:** 1 January – 30 June
- **[2024 Scheme only]** A **Summer Semester** (typically May–June) is also part of the academic year structure, mainly for internships/additional skill courses.

Every registered course is evaluated through two components:

| Component | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)** | Day-to-day performance: internal tests, assignments, attendance, class work, quizzes, case studies, etc., conducted by the college. |
| **End Semester Examination (ESE)** | A University-conducted, centrally set written/practical examination held at the end of the semester. |

**CIE : ESE weightage ratio:**

| Course Type | 2019 Scheme | 2024 Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Theory | 1 : 2 | 2 : 3 |
| Laboratory/Practical | 1 : 1 | 1 : 1 |
| PBL (Project-Based Learning) | Not applicable | 3 : 2 |
| HMC (Humanities/Management) | Not applicable | 1 : 1 |
| Project | CIE only | CIE only |
| Seminar | CIE only | CIE only |
| Internship | Not separately specified | CIE only |

ESE is held **twice a year** — the **May/June session** (even semesters) and the **November/December session** (odd semesters). Semesters VII and VIII have ESE in **both** sessions.

**Difference between CIE and ESE, in plain terms:** CIE reflects a student's regular classroom performance across the semester and is entirely within the college's control (subject to University audit); ESE is a one-time, University-set and University-conducted examination at the end of the semester that carries the larger share of the final grade for theory/lab courses.

## 4. HALL TICKET / ADMIT CARD

*Source: KTU Examination Manual 2022 (as reproduced in KTU-affiliated college exam-cell instructions)*

- **Purpose:** The hall ticket is the University-issued authorization to sit a specific ESE; it confirms the candidate's registration for that course/session.
- **How obtained:** Generated on the **KTU student portal** after successful exam registration and fee payment. (Exact download steps on the CET-specific portal interface: **Not Available (CET-specific)** — refer to the KTU portal help/CET Examination Office.)
- **When available:** Typically shortly before the ESE date; **exact release date: TBA per exam cycle**.
- **What it displays:** Candidate details, registered courses for that session, and exam centre/session information (exact field list: **Not Available**).
- **If information is incorrect:** Report immediately to the College Exam Cell / KTU for correction before the exam date. (Specific correction procedure: **Not Available (CET-specific) — students should contact the CET Examination Office.**)
- **If lost:** A candidate **cannot** write the exam without a valid hall ticket under normal circumstances. However, the Examination Manual permits the **Chief/Deputy Superintendent to grant provisional admission** on payment of a University-specified fine, provided the candidate's name appears in the registered-candidates list and eligibility is confirmed. The candidate must submit a provisional-admission application.
- **Required identification:** The hall ticket **and** a photo ID (college ID card or other valid photo ID) must both be carried and produced on demand to the Chief/Deputy Superintendent, observer, squad member, or any University-authorized officer.

## 5. EXAMINATION DAY PROCEDURE

*Source: KTU Examination Manual 2022 ("B.Tech Degree Examinations — Instructions to Candidates")*

### Before Leaving — Checklist
- Hall ticket (mandatory, every exam day)
- Photo ID card (college ID or other valid photo ID)
- Permitted stationery/drawing equipment and accessories
- Non-programmable calculator (**only if permitted** for that specific exam)
- Permitted reference material (IS codes/Data books/Tables/Handbook), **only if allowed for that exam and with no extra writing/scribbling in them**
- Arrive at least **10 minutes before** commencement

### At the Examination Centre
- Candidates presenting themselves **more than 30 minutes after** commencement will **not be admitted**.
- Hall ticket and photo ID must be produced on demand to invigilators/superintendents/observers/squad members.
- Candidates occupy their allotted seats; identity is verified against the hall ticket and registered-candidate list.

### During the Examination
**Permitted:**
- Hall ticket, valid photo ID
- Drawing equipment and accessories
- Non-programmable calculator (only if the specific exam allows it)
- IS codes/Data books/Tables/Handbooks specifically permitted by the University, free of any additional writing

**Prohibited:**
- All electronic gadgets — mobile phones, tablets, iPads, smartwatches, programmable calculators, headsets/earphones, Bluetooth devices, pen cameras, or any communication/storage device
- Books, printed material, or manuscript material, other than what is explicitly permitted
- Writing on the hall ticket or question paper
- Revealing identity anywhere in the answer script, or appealing to examiners for leniency within the script
- Communicating with any other candidate or anyone outside the hall

**Leaving the hall:**
- No candidate may leave within the first **150 minutes** of the exam, nor re-enter once they have left.
- A candidate leaving early must return the question paper to the invigilator.

### After the Examination
- The answer book must be **handed over to the invigilator personally** — candidates must not leave it on their seat and walk out.
- Retain: hall ticket (for subsequent exam days) and any personal belongings collected before entry.

### Use of Scribes
Available for **blind candidates** and candidates with a disability that prevents writing, on the recommendation of a government medical officer (not below the rank of Civil Surgeon) and approval of the Chief/Deputy Superintendent. Also available in genuine **medical emergencies**, on similar certification. A prescribed application with the scribe's declaration must reach the Controller of Examinations' office **at least seven working days before** the exam.

## 7. PROJECT / SEMINAR / INTERNSHIP EVALUATION

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.4; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.3, R7.6*

- **Project:** Evaluated entirely through CIE (no separate ESE). **[2019 Scheme]** Weightage: guide assessment 30%, 3-member CIE committee 40%, final evaluation by a 3-member committee (department project coordinator, guide, external expert — industry expert preferred) 30%. One-third of project credit is normally completed in Semester VII and two-thirds in Semester VIII.
- **Seminar:** CIE only. **[2019 Scheme]** Weightage: Attendance 10%, Guide 20%, Technical content 30%, Presentation 40%, evaluated by a team of three senior internal faculty. **[2024 Scheme]** Students doing an internship away from their home college may deliver the Semester VII seminar online, evaluated on the same criteria as in-person seminars.
- **Internship [2024 Scheme]:** Students undertaking an internship in Semester VII or VIII may opt for a self-study/self-learning mode instead of regular classes; evaluation mirrors that of regular theory courses, and the internship attendance period counts as equivalent to class attendance. All standard course requirements (assignments, micro-projects, internal exams) still apply as instructed by the course faculty/HoD.

## 8. MARKS AND GRADING

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.12, R6.16, R6.21; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.17, R7.21, R9.1–R9.4*

**Pass criteria (both schemes):**
- Minimum **40%** in the ESE, **and**
- Minimum **50%** in the combined CIE + ESE score.
- Falling below either threshold results in an **F** grade for that course.
- **[2024]** For courses assessed **solely** by CIE (no ESE — e.g. Project, Seminar), the pass minimum is **50%** of CIE; failing this earns an **FE** grade (not F).

**Grade Table (identical grade points in both schemes):**

| Grade | Grade Point | % of Total Marks | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| S | 10.0 | 90% and above | Outstanding |
| A+ | 9.0 | 85% – <90% | Excellent |
| A | 8.5 | 80% – <85% | Very Good |
| B+ | 8.0 | 75% – <80% | Good |
| B | 7.5 | 70% – <75% | Above Average |
| C+ | 7.0 | 65% – <70% | Average |
| C | 6.5 | 60% – <65% | Satisfactory |
| D | 6.0 | 55% – <60% | Marginal |
| P (Pass) | 5.5 | 50% – <55% | Minimum Pass |
| F (Fail) | 0 | Below 50% overall, or below 40% in ESE | Failed |
| FE (Failed – Eligibility) | 0 | Attendance/eligibility criteria not met (also CIE-only course failure in 2024 Scheme) | Not eligible / failed on eligibility |
| I **[2019 Scheme]** | 0 | Could not appear for ESE but met eligibility criteria | Incomplete |
| Ab (Absent) **[2024 Scheme]** | 0 | Deferred or absent from ESE despite meeting eligibility | Absent |

**Passing grades (credit-earning):** S, A+, A, B+, B, C+, C, D, P.

**Degree Classification:**

| Classification | 2019 Scheme | 2024 Scheme |
|---|---|---|
| First Class with Distinction | CGPA ≥ 8.0 | CGPA ≥ 8.0 |
| First Class | CGPA ≥ 6.5 | 6.5 ≤ CGPA < 8.0 |

**Equivalent Percentage — regulation-dependent, do not conflate:**
- **[2019 Scheme]** Equivalent % = **(10 × CGPA) − 2.5**
- **[2024 Scheme]** Equivalent % = **10 × CGPA** (no subtraction)

**SGPA/CGPA formulas (both schemes, same structure):**

```
SGPA = Σ(Ci × GPi) / ΣCi   — summed over all courses registered in that semester
CGPA = Σ(Ci × GPi) / ΣCi   — summed over all courses up to that point in the programme
```
Where Ci = credits for a course, GPi = grade point earned. **F, FE, I/Ab grades are all included (as zero grade points) in both SGPA and CGPA calculations** — a backlog pulls down SGPA/CGPA until cleared. **[2024]** GPA/CGPA are calculated to two decimal places; credits from Minor, Honours, and Challenge-exam courses are excluded from the main B.Tech SGPA/CGPA.

**Absent vs Failed status:**
- **[2019]** "I" grade = student completed the course (met eligibility) but did not sit the ESE; must register and appear at the next opportunity.
- **[2024]** "Ab" grade = similar concept; if the student does not appear at the very next available (supplementary) opportunity, the Ab grade **converts to an F grade**. In Semester VIII, any absence is recorded directly as F, regardless of reason.

## 9. EXAMINATION MALPRACTICE

*Source: KTU Examination Manual 2022, as reproduced in official KTU-affiliated college exam-cell "Instructions to Candidates" documents*

**What constitutes malpractice (illustrative, not exhaustive):**
- Possessing unauthorized material in the exam hall (written notes, marked hall tickets, textbook photocopies, marked instruments/clothing, etc.)
- Copying from or exchanging answers with another candidate, with or without mutual consent
- Possession of any electronic/communication device (mobile phone, smartwatch, Bluetooth device, etc.)
- Impersonation
- Manhandling or threatening invigilators/officials
- Tampering with answer books (own or another's), destroying evidence of malpractice, running away with the answer script
- Communicating exam content outside the hall through any electronic/cyber medium
- Smuggling question papers or exam materials out of the hall
- Mass copying involving more than 3 students
- Disturbing candidates from outside the hall, breaking into a sealed exam hall, stealing answer books

**Investigation process:** Malpractice observed or reported by any authorized official is examined; the candidate has the right to be heard before the college's **Examination Malpractice Committee / Disciplinary Action Committee (EMC/DAC)**, and may appeal to the **University's Examination Appeals Committee**. A candidate caught for malpractice may still be permitted to sit the remaining scheduled exams (a fresh answer book is issued for the ongoing exam if applicable).

**Illustrative consequences (severity increases with the nature and repetition of the offence):**

| Nature of offence (examples) | Typical penalty range |
|---|---|
| Possessing non-permissible material; copying by peeping; helping/threatening a neighbour to show script | Debarment from all courses in that examination session + fine (~₹2,000; **verify current amount**) |
| Possessing large quantities of unauthorized material; possessing an electronic gadget; tampering with barcodes/register numbers; possessing photocopied notes/textbook material | Debarment for **2 chances** for that course, plus debarment from all courses in that exam session + fine |
| Impersonation; manhandling/threatening officials; tampering with another candidate's script; smuggling papers; communicating exam content via electronic media | **Permanent debarring**, case referred to police |
| Mass copying (more than 3 students) | Debarment for 2 chances for the course + debarment from all courses in that session + fine; institutional-level inquiry may follow |
| Repeat offences (2nd, 3rd, 4th time) | Escalating penalties from debarment of all courses in that semester (incl. supplementary) up to **permanent debarring** with fines up to ~₹30,000 (**verify current amounts**) |

**Relevant authority:** College-level **EMC/DAC**, escalating to the **Controller of Examinations (CoE)** and the **University's Examination Appeals Committee** for serious cases. Exact current penalty amounts should be verified against the latest KTU Examination Manual, as fine amounts are periodically revised.

## 10. ABSENCE FROM EXAMINATION

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.5; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.16*

- A student who has **completed a course and registered for the ESE** but does not sit it (for any reason, including health/personal emergencies) is awarded an **"I" grade [2019]** or **"Ab" grade [2024]**, provided the minimum attendance/eligibility criteria were otherwise met.
- The **next available ESE opportunity is treated as the student's first attempt** at that exam.
- **[2024]** If the student does not register for and attend the **immediate next** opportunity, the Ab grade automatically converts to an **F** grade.
- **Semester VIII exception [2024]:** A student **cannot defer** the Semester VIII ESE; non-attendance in Semester VIII results directly in an **F** grade regardless of the reason.
- **Honours/Minor courses [2024]:** Non-attendance at the ESE for an Honours or Minor course results directly in an **F** grade, regardless of reason.
- **Medical emergencies / documentation:** No separate University-wide "special/emergency exam" process beyond the I/Ab-grade-and-next-opportunity mechanism was found in the regulations reviewed. Students facing a genuine emergency should **contact the CET Examination Office / their Faculty Advisor immediately**, and — where a break of study is warranted (e.g., prolonged illness) — may apply for a formal **Break of Study** (see Regulations R7 [2019]/ separately documented), which requires medical reports and, on return, a fitness certificate.
- **Who to contact:** Faculty Advisor/Senior Faculty Advisor in the first instance, escalating to the HoD, Principal, and the University Controller of Examinations as needed. (CET-specific emergency-contact procedure: **Not Available.**)

## 12. REVALUATION

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.19; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.32 (procedural authority delegated to the University Examination Manual); KTU portal notifications (illustrative/historical figures)*

**What it means:** A student who is not satisfied with their ESE mark may apply to have the answer script **re-evaluated** by another examiner.

**Eligibility:** Any student, for any ESE course, after results are declared (not restricted to failed courses — students may also seek revaluation of a passed course if they believe the mark is wrong).

**Procedure and outcome (per Regulation R6.19, 2019 Scheme — the substantive rule; the same principle is carried forward and administered via the University Examination Manual under the 2024 Scheme):**
1. Apply for revaluation via the **KTU student portal**, within the window announced by the University for that examination cycle.
2. The script is revalued by another examiner.
3. **Final mark = the better of the original and revalued marks.**
4. If the difference between the original and revalued marks exceeds **15% of the maximum marks**, the script is sent for a **third valuation**.
5. In that case, the final mark = the **average of the two closer valuations** (whichever benefits the student) **or** the original mark, **whichever is higher**.
6. The Controller of Examinations may investigate cases of suspected negligent valuation and take action against the examiner concerned.

**Application procedure:** Register and pay the revaluation fee through the **KTU student portal** (or, in some cycles, through the college office) within the notified window.

**Fee:** Revaluation fees have historically been in the approximate range of **₹500–₹650 per answer script** in past KTU notifications (2018–2023 cycles) — **this is illustrative/historical data only; the exact current fee is set afresh for each examination cycle and must be verified from the live KTU portal notification. Do not quote a fixed figure to students as current.**

**Deadline:** Application window is announced per examination cycle by the University — **exact number of days after result publication: TBA (verify against the specific KTU notification for that exam).**

| Step | Action | Requirement | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Register for revaluation on KTU portal | Result must be published | Within the University-notified window (**TBA per cycle**) |
| 2 | Pay revaluation fee | Prescribed fee per script (**TBA — verify current amount**) | Same window |
| 3 | Script re-evaluated by another examiner | — | Per University processing schedule |
| 4 | Result declared | — | Per University schedule |
| 5 (if applicable) | Third valuation, if difference > 15% of max marks | Automatic, triggered by the University | Per University schedule |

## 13. ANSWER-SCRIPT SCRUTINY / VERIFICATION ("Review" and Photocopy)

*Source: KTU portal "Review Request" student-help documentation (official KTU procedural guide)*

KTU's process for a **second-level check after revaluation** is called **"Review."** This is distinct from Revaluation (Section 12):

- **Revaluation** = the script is examined again by a different examiner from scratch.
- **Review** = a student who has **failed even after revaluation** requests that the University examine their case again, typically after obtaining a certified **photocopy** of their evaluated answer script to identify a specific concern.

**Eligibility:** Students who have **failed after revaluation** (UG) — or, for PG, failed after the original valuation.

**Procedure:**
1. **If the student already has a photocopy** of the answer script (obtained via the portal): apply for Review within **15 calendar days** of the revaluation result being declared.
2. **If the student does not yet have a photocopy:** first apply for the photocopy within **7 calendar days** of the revaluation result being declared, then apply for Review within **7–8 days** after the photocopy is made available.
3. Fee payment is made online through the KTU portal (or receipt submitted to the designated review email address, historically `review_ug@ktu.edu.in` for UG students — **verify this address is still current before sharing with students**).

**Fee:** Historically higher than ordinary revaluation (illustrative past figure: **~₹5,000** for Review, with a partial refund — historically ~₹3,000 — **only if the grade actually changes**, plus an additional photocopy fee if not already obtained). **These are historical/illustrative figures from past KTU cycles — verify current amounts on the KTU portal before advising students.**

**What is checked:** Whether the revaluation itself was conducted correctly / whether the University-level assessment stands, based on the documented answer script.

**Difference from Revaluation:** Revaluation is a **fresh, independent re-marking**; Review is a **check on the revaluation process itself**, available only after a failed revaluation attempt, and carries a distinct (typically higher) fee and separate deadline.

## 14. SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMINATIONS

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.2, R6.13; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.2, R7.19*

**What it means at KTU:** KTU does **not** run a separate, differently-numbered "supplementary exam" system in the way some universities do. Instead, the **End Semester Examination itself is held twice a year** (May/June and Nov/Dec sessions), and a student who has an **F** grade (or FE, once re-registered) simply **appears again at the next available ESE session** for that course — this next-opportunity attempt is what is commonly referred to as the "supplementary" exam.

**Eligibility:** Any student with an outstanding **F** grade (both schemes) or a re-registered **FE**-grade course.

**Registration procedure:**
- **F grade [both schemes]:** Student must **register for the ESE again** at the next opportunity. **The course itself does not need to be re-registered** — only the exam registration.
- **FE grade:** Student must **re-register for the course** in a semester when it is normally offered (2019 Scheme); under the 2024 Scheme, "trailing" students (past the normal 8-semester duration) may register for FE courses in **any** semester the college offers them, without the standard per-semester credit cap.

**Syllabus continuity [2024 Scheme, R7.2]:** If a course's syllabus has been revised, students attempting supplementary/next-opportunity exams may continue under the **original syllabus for up to two supplementary attempts**; from the **third attempt onward**, they must sit the exam on the **revised syllabus**.

**Grace Marks and supplementary attempts:** **Grace Marks are NOT awarded for supplementary examinations** [2019 Scheme, R13.11] — grace marks apply only to the regular/performing-semester examination.

**Fee:** As per standard exam registration fee for the relevant semester (**exact amount: TBA**).

**Restrictions:** Maximum permissible programme duration is **12 semesters (6 academic years)** from admission [2019 Scheme, R3.2] or a **4-year normal duration + 2-year grace period** [2024 Scheme, R4.1], after which registration is automatically cancelled unless a University extension is granted.

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| Who can appear | Any student with an outstanding F (both schemes) or re-registered FE grade |
| Which courses | Only the specific failed/backlog course(s) |
| Registration | Exam-only re-registration for F grade; full course re-registration for FE grade |
| Grace marks | Not awarded for supplementary attempts |
| Syllabus | Original syllabus honoured for up to 2 supplementary attempts [2024]; revised syllabus thereafter |
| Maximum duration | 12 semesters / 6 years [2019]; 4 years + 2-year grace [2024] |

## 15. IMPROVEMENT EXAMINATIONS

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.18; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.28*

**KTU B.Tech regulations do NOT provide for grade-improvement examinations.** This applies under **both** the 2019 and 2024 schemes:

- **[2019 Scheme, R6.18]:** *"There is no provision for improving the grade."* A student may, however, apply to see (via revaluation/photocopy) the answer books of the ESE, on payment of the prescribed fee, if they suspect an evaluation error — but this is a **correction mechanism (Section 12), not a grade-improvement mechanism.**
- **[2024 Scheme, R7.28]:** *"Students are not permitted to improve the grades of a passed or successfully completed course."*

**Practical implication for students:** If a student has **already passed** a course and wants a higher grade, **there is no official route to retake that exam for grade improvement.** The only recourse for a suspected marking error on an already-passed course is **Revaluation** (Section 12), which can only correct an error — it is not designed to allow "another attempt" at a better score through fresh preparation.

**One narrow exception [2024 Scheme, R7.8]:** The **University itself** — not the student — may, in exceptional cases of proven **procedural lapses or major discrepancies** in the internal assessment process affecting an entire class, direct a re-assessment of CIE marks. This is not a student-initiated improvement option.

## 16. BACKLOGS / FAILED COURSES

*Source: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2019 R6.13, R6.17, R3.2; KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024 R7.14, R7.19, R7.29, R4.1*

**Definition:**
- **Failing a course** = receiving an **F** or **FE** grade for a single course in a single semester's ESE attempt.
- **Having a backlog** = the *state* of having **one or more unresolved F/FE-graded courses** from any earlier semester, still pending clearance. A "trailing student" [2024 term] is one who has completed the normal 8-semester duration but still carries backlog(s).

**What happens after failing a course:**
1. The course remains **incomplete/unpassed**; its zero grade point is included in SGPA/CGPA calculations until cleared.
2. The student must clear it via the **next available ESE opportunity** (Section 14) — for F grade, exam-only re-registration; for FE grade, full course re-registration when next offered.
3. **[2019]** No re-registration of the course itself is allowed for an F grade — only re-appearing at the ESE.
4. **[2024]** Trailing students may register FE-grade backlog courses from **any** semester in which the institute offers them, exempt from the normal semester credit cap.

Unearned-credit courses are assigned a zero grade point, which lowers CGPA but does not, by itself, block graduation once the 160-credit floor is met.

## 17. REVALUATION vs SUPPLEMENTARY vs IMPROVEMENT

| Option | Purpose | Who Can Apply | When Used | Result Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Revaluation** | Re-check a specific answer script by another examiner for a possible marking error | Any student, for any ESE course (pass or fail), after results are declared | After result publication, within the notified window | Final mark = better of original/revalued (or the special third-valuation rule if the gap exceeds 15% of max marks) |
| **Supplementary (next-opportunity) exam** | Clear a **failed (F)** course, or a course where eligibility was not met (FE) | Any student with an F/FE grade | At the next ESE session (twice yearly) | Course is passed if the new attempt meets the pass criteria; grade reflects new performance |
| **Improvement** | **Not available at KTU** — no mechanism exists to retake a course already passed, purely to raise the grade | N/A | N/A | N/A |

**Simple decision guide:**
- **If you believe your mark is incorrect →** Apply for **Revaluation** (Section 12); if you still disagree after revaluation, apply for **Review** (Section 13).
- **If you failed a course →** Register again for the **next ESE opportunity** ("supplementary") — Section 14.
- **If you want to improve an existing (already-passed) result →** **Not possible under KTU regulations** (Section 15). Consider whether Revaluation applies if you believe there was an actual marking error, not just a wish for a higher score.
- **If you missed an examination →** You will normally receive an **I/Ab grade** (Section 10) and must register for the **next available opportunity** as your first attempt.

## 18. COMMON STUDENT PROBLEMS

### "I have attendance shortage. Can I write the exam?"
You need **≥75% attendance** per course to be eligible for the ESE. If your attendance is between 60–75% and you have genuine (e.g., medical) grounds, the **Principal may condone** it — for a maximum of two semesters across your whole programme (2019 Scheme), or subject to a prescribed condonation fee (2024 Scheme). Below 60%, condonation is generally not available under the standard provisions reviewed. Approach your Faculty Advisor/Principal well before the exam registration deadline.

### "I failed a subject. What can I do?"
You carry an **F** grade; there's no separate registration needed for the course itself — just **re-register for the ESE** at the next opportunity (May/June or Nov/Dec session). See Section 14.

### "I think my marks are wrong."
Apply for **Revaluation** through the KTU portal within the notified window after results. If you fail even after revaluation, you can apply for **Review**, typically after obtaining a photocopy of your script. See Sections 12–13.

### "I missed my examination."
If you had completed the course and were registered but didn't sit the exam, you'll typically get an **I/Ab grade**, and the **next available exam session becomes your first attempt**. Note: under the 2024 Scheme, if you don't take that very next opportunity, the Ab grade converts to an F. Semester VIII absence is always recorded as F, regardless of reason.

### "I forgot my hall ticket."
Under standard rules, you cannot write the exam without a valid hall ticket. The Chief/Deputy Superintendent **may** grant provisional admission on payment of a University-specified fine if you're on the registered-candidates list — you'll need to apply for this on the spot. Contact your college's Examination Cell immediately.

### "I entered the wrong course during registration."
Once ESE registration is complete, course changes are generally **not permitted**, with narrow exceptions for FE-grade elective students whose original elective is no longer offered (2024 Scheme). Contact your Faculty Advisor/HoD as early as possible — the exact CET-level correction process is **Not Available (CET-specific)**; escalate to the Examination Office.

### "I missed the examination registration deadline."
Late registration provisions, if any, are **not detailed** in the University-level regulations reviewed. Contact the **CET Examination Office** immediately — do not wait, as missing registration means you cannot appear for that session's ESE for the affected course(s).

### "My result shows an incorrect grade."
This is typically addressed through **Revaluation** (Section 12) or, for a clerical/portal error (not a marking dispute), by contacting the Controller of Examinations through your Principal.

### "I want to improve my grade."
KTU regulations **do not permit grade improvement** for an already-passed course (Section 15). If you suspect an actual evaluation error, Revaluation is the correct route — not a route to a "better" score through general re-preparation.

### "I have multiple backlogs."
Each backlog course must be cleared individually via the next ESE opportunity for that course. Be mindful of the **minimum cumulative credit requirements** for promotion to higher semesters (Section 16) and the **maximum programme duration** (12 semesters/6 years under the 2019 Scheme; 4 years + 2-year grace under the 2024 Scheme). Speak with your Faculty Advisor/SFA to plan a clearance sequence.

## 22. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**General Examination**
1. **What is an end-semester examination?** A University-conducted written/practical exam held at the end of each semester, evaluating the bulk of a course's marks alongside CIE.
2. **What are the examination requirements to sit an ESE?** ≥75% attendance in that course, completed exam registration, and no pending disciplinary action.
3. **What is internal assessment (CIE)?** Continuous evaluation through tests, assignments, attendance and class work carried out by the college throughout the semester.
4. **How many times a year is the ESE held?** Twice — May/June (even semesters) and Nov/Dec (odd semesters); Semesters VII/VIII in both sessions.
5. **What is the CIE:ESE ratio for theory courses?** 1:2 [2019 Scheme] or 2:3 [2024 Scheme].
6. **What is the CIE:ESE ratio for lab courses?** 1:1 in both schemes.
7. **Is Project evaluated by ESE?** No, CIE only.
8. **Is Seminar evaluated by ESE?** No, CIE only.

**Registration**
9. **How do I register for an exam?** Complete Course Registration and then separate Exam (ESE) Registration on the KTU portal within the announced window, and pay the fee.
10. **What happens if I miss registration?** You cannot appear for that session's ESE for the affected course(s); contact the CET Examination Office immediately.
11. **Can I register for backlog courses?** Yes — F-grade courses need exam-only re-registration; FE-grade courses need full course re-registration when offered.
12. **Can I change my elective after registration?** Generally no, once ESE registration is complete, with narrow FE-related exceptions (2024 Scheme).

**Examination Day**
13. **What should I bring?** Hall ticket, photo ID, and only exam-specific permitted materials.
14. **Can I use a calculator?** Only a **non-programmable** calculator, and only if that specific exam permits it.
15. **What happens if I arrive late?** You will not be admitted if you arrive more than 30 minutes after commencement.
16. **Can I leave early?** Not within the first 150 minutes; if leaving early after that, you must return the question paper.
17. **Are mobile phones allowed?** No — strictly prohibited, along with all electronic/communication devices.

**Results**
18. **How is SGPA calculated?** SGPA = Σ(Ci×GPi)/ΣCi for all courses registered that semester, including failed/incomplete ones.
19. **What is CGPA?** The cumulative, credit-weighted grade point average across all semesters completed so far.
20. **What happens if I fail?** You receive an F grade; the course's zero grade point counts in SGPA/CGPA until you clear it at the next opportunity.
21. **When does CGPA appear on my grade card?** Only once you have no outstanding backlogs at that point (2024 Scheme).
22. **What CGPA is needed for First Class?** ≥6.5 (both schemes; capped below 8.0 for 2024 Scheme's First Class band).
23. **What CGPA is needed for First Class with Distinction?** ≥8.0 (both schemes).
24. **How do I convert CGPA to a percentage?** (10×CGPA − 2.5) under the 2019 Scheme; (10×CGPA) under the 2024 Scheme — the formulas differ, so check which scheme applies to you.

**Revaluation**
25. **What is revaluation?** A re-check of your answer script by a different examiner.
26. **Who can apply?** Any student, for any ESE course, after results are published.
27. **How do I apply?** Through the KTU student portal, within the notified window, with the prescribed fee.
28. **What happens after revaluation?** You get the better of your original and revalued marks; if the two differ by more than 15% of max marks, a third valuation is triggered.
29. **What if I still disagree after revaluation?** You may apply for a Review, generally after obtaining a photocopy of your script.
30. **Is there a deadline for revaluation?** Yes, announced per exam cycle — check the current KTU notification.

**Supplementary**
31. **What is a supplementary examination at KTU?** The next available regular ESE session, which a student with an F/FE grade attends to clear the backlog — KTU does not run a separately branded supplementary exam.
32. **Who can write it?** Any student with an outstanding F/FE grade in a course.
33. **Can I clear a backlog through the next-opportunity exam?** Yes — that is the standard mechanism.
34. **Are grace marks given in supplementary attempts?** No — grace marks apply only to the regular performing-semester exam.
35. **Does the syllabus change for supplementary attempts?** You may continue on the original syllabus for up to 2 supplementary attempts (2024 Scheme); from the 3rd attempt, the revised syllabus applies.

**Improvement**
36. **Can I improve my grade in a passed course?** No — KTU regulations explicitly do not allow this, under both schemes.
37. **Which courses are eligible for improvement?** None — the provision does not exist.
38. **What if I think my "passed" grade was still wrongly calculated?** Use Revaluation, not "improvement" — these are different mechanisms.

**Special Cases**
39. **What happens if I miss an exam?** You typically receive an I/Ab grade and must attend the next available session as your first attempt.
40. **What if my result is incorrect due to a portal/clerical error?** Contact the CET Examination Office and Controller of Examinations, separate from the revaluation process.
41. **What if I have attendance shortage?** You may seek Principal-level condonation (up to two semesters across your programme, subject to conditions/fee).
42. **What if I have a medical emergency during exam season?** Contact your Faculty Advisor/Principal immediately; a scribe may be arranged for a medical emergency that prevents writing, and a formal Break of Study may be considered for prolonged illness.
43. **Can I use a scribe?** Yes, for blind candidates, candidates with a writing-preventing disability, or genuine medical emergencies, subject to certification and a 7-working-day advance application.
44. **What is an FE grade?** Failure due to not meeting eligibility criteria (mainly attendance), or CIE-only course failure (2024 Scheme) — distinct from an ordinary F grade.
45. **What is an Ab/I grade?** Awarded when a student meets eligibility but does not sit the ESE; treated as their "first attempt" being deferred to the next session.
46. **How many backlogs can I have and still continue?** No fixed backlog-count cap was found in the regulations reviewed; the binding constraints are the minimum cumulative credit requirements per semester (Section 16) and maximum programme duration.
47. **What is the maximum time to complete my B.Tech?** 6 years/12 semesters (2019 Scheme) or 4 years + 2-year grace period (2024 Scheme).
48. **What happens if I don't clear backlogs within the maximum duration?** Registration is automatically cancelled unless the University grants an extension.
49. **Can I graduate with some credits still short?** Yes, under the 2024 Scheme's Credit Relaxation Policy, up to 10 credits' worth may remain short (except Seminar/Mini Project/Project-Internship), with a zero grade point impact on CGPA.
50. **Who do I contact first for any examination problem?** Your Faculty Advisor/Senior Faculty Advisor, who will escalate to the HoD, Principal, or CET Examination Office as needed.

## 23. TERMINOLOGY / GLOSSARY

- **Internal Assessment (CIE):** Continuous Internal Evaluation — the college-conducted component of a course's total marks, based on tests, assignments, attendance, and class work.
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE):** The University-conducted examination held at the end of each semester.
- **Hall Ticket:** The University-issued document authorizing a student to sit a specific examination.
- **Revaluation:** Re-checking of an answer script by a different examiner, at the student's request and expense.
- **Scrutiny/Review:** At KTU, the "Review" process — a further check requested after a student has already failed a revaluation attempt.
- **Supplementary Examination:** Colloquial term for the next-opportunity ESE session a student attends to clear an F/FE grade; not a separately branded exam type at KTU.
- **Improvement Examination:** Not available under KTU regulations — no mechanism to retake a passed course purely to raise the grade.
- **Backlog:** An unresolved F/FE-graded course from a previous semester.
- **SGPA:** Semester Grade Point Average — the credit-weighted average grade point for one semester.
- **CGPA:** Cumulative Grade Point Average — the credit-weighted average grade point across all semesters to date.
- **Grade Point:** The numeric value (0–10) assigned to each letter grade.
- **Credit:** The unit of academic weight assigned to a course based on contact hours.
- **Malpractice:** Any act of unfair means, indiscipline, or misconduct in connection with an examination.
- **Semester:** A six-month academic term (Odd: July–December; Even: January–June); the 2024 Scheme adds a Summer Semester.
- **Course Registration:** The process of enrolling in a course for a semester.
- **Exam Registration:** The separate process of registering to sit the ESE for a registered course.
- **FE Grade:** "Failed due to lack of Eligibility" — awarded when attendance/eligibility criteria are not met (or for CIE-only course failure under the 2024 Scheme).
- **F Grade:** Ordinary fail — ESE and/or combined score below the pass threshold.
- **I / Ab Grade:** Awarded when a student meets eligibility but does not sit the ESE (2019 Scheme: "I"; 2024 Scheme: "Ab").
