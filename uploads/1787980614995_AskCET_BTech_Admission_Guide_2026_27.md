## 2. ADMISSION ROUTE

B.Tech admissions to CET for the 2026–27 academic year follow multi-tiered, officially recognized routes. There is **no direct management or private quota** in CET, as it is a pure Government institution.

```
[Candidate]
   │
   ├──> 1. KEAM Route (Primary Route - 100% Regular State Merit & Reservation Seats)
   │       │
   │       ├──> CEE Kerala KEAM Computer-Based Test (CBT) / Rank List
   │       └──> Single Window Centralized Allotment Process (CAP)
   │
   ├──> 2. B.Tech Lateral Entry Route (Admission directly to 2nd Year / 3rd Semester)
   │       │
   │       ├──> Diploma in Engineering / B.Sc Degree Holders
   │       └──> State-Level Entrance Test conducted by DTE / CEE Kerala
   │
   └──> 3. Special Supernumerary Categories (As mandated by AICTE/Govt)
           ├──> Persons with Disabilities (PwD) - via CEE Rank
           ├──> Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Scheme - via CEE Rank & Income Criteria
           └──> Nominees of Govt of India / Defense Personnel (where allotted by CEE)
```

### 2.1 Primary Route: KEAM Centralized Allotment Process (CAP)
1. **Entrance Test:** Candidates must qualify for the Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical (KEAM 2026) entrance examination conducted by CEE Kerala.
2. **Normalized Score & Rank:** CEE publishes an Engineering Rank List computed based on KEAM scores combined with Qualifying Examination (10+2) marks as per CEE rules.
3. **Single Window CAP:** Seat allotment is strictly executed via CEE Kerala's automated Centralized Allotment Process (CAP) based on candidate ranks, category reservations, and web options registered by the applicant.
4. **College Reporting:** Candidates receiving an allotment to CET report physically to the college for document verification and final admission confirmation.

## 4. KEAM / ENTRANCE EXAMINATION

### 4.1 What is KEAM?
KEAM (Kerala Engineering Architecture Medical) is the state-level entrance examination conducted annually by the **Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Government of Kerala**, to determine merit for admission to professional degree courses across Kerala.

### 4.2 Examination Structure & Role
- **Mode:** Computer-Based Test (CBT) or offline OMR-based test as declared by CEE for the 2026 cycle.
- **Subjects Evaluated:** Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- **Rank Preparation:** The Engineering Rank list is prepared by assigning equal weightage (typically 50:50) to:
  1. The marks secured in the KEAM 2026 Engineering Entrance Examination.
  2. The standardized marks obtained in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry/Optional subject in the Qualifying Class 12 Examination.
- **Mandatory Requirement:** A valid KEAM 2026 rank is mandatory for securing a seat at CET under all regular state categories.

9. **Step 9: Physical Reporting & Verification at CET**
   - *Authority:* College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET)
   - Report in person to the CET campus on designated dates with all original certificates, verification forms, and balance fees.

10. **Step 10: Final Admission Confirmation & KTU Enrollment**
    - *Authority:* CET & KTU

## 7. ALLOTMENT PROCESS

The allotment of B.Tech seats at CET is entirely automated and managed by CEE Kerala via the Centralized Allotment Process (CAP).

### 7.1 Option Registration Rules
- Candidates must register their choices of colleges and branches on the CEE online portal (`www.cee.kerala.gov.in`).
- CET is identified by the college code **TVE**.
- Candidates can choose options such as:
  - `TVE - Computer Science & Engineering (CS)`
  - `TVE - Electronics & Communication Engineering (EC)`
  - `TVE - Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EE)`
  - `TVE - Mechanical Engineering (ME)`
  - `TVE - Civil Engineering (CE)`
- **Order of Preference:** Options must be entered strictly in order of personal preference. If a candidate is allotted an option high on their preference list, lower options are automatically cancelled.

### 7.2 Allotment Rounds & Upgradation
1. **Phase 1 Allotment:** Based on rank, nativity, and reservation rules. Candidates getting allotment must pay the CEE fee to secure the seat.
2. **Higher Option Retention:** Candidates who receive an allotment at CET or another college but wish to be considered for higher preferences in subsequent rounds must retain their higher options on the portal.
3. **Phase 2 & Subsequent Allotments:** If a higher preference option becomes available at CET, the system automatically upgrades the allotment. Once upgraded, the previously held lower seat is relinquished immediately and allotted to another candidate.
4. **Final Confirmation:** Upon completion of the allotment phases as announced by CEE, candidates must physically report to CET to complete enrollment.

## 9. RESERVATION / SEAT CATEGORIES

CET is a Government institution and follows the official mandatory reservation policy framed by the Government of Kerala.

### 9.1 Seat Breakdown Categories

1. **State Merit (SM):** Open for candidates based purely on KEAM rank, irrespective of community, provided nativity criteria (Keralite) are met.
2. **Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC):** Category-wise reservation for backward communities including Ezhava (EZ), Muslim (MU), Other Backward Hindus (BH), Latin Catholic & Anglo Indian (LA), Dheevara (DV), Viswakarma (VK), Other Backward Christians (BX), and Kudumbi (KU). *(Requires valid Non-Creamy Layer Certificate).*
3. **Scheduled Castes (SC) & Scheduled Tribes (ST):** Reserved seats as per government norms.
4. **Economically Weaker Sections (EWS):** Seats allocated for candidates belonging to forward communities who satisfy state EWS income/land criteria.
5. **Persons with Disabilities (PwD):** Supernumerary/reserved seats allocated to benchmark disabled candidates as per CEE guidelines.
6. **Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Scheme:** AICTE-approved supernumerary seats (typically 5% over and above sanctioned intake) allocated by CEE to high-merit candidates from low-income families.

*Note: Exact reservation percentages for the 2026–27 session are published in the official KEAM 2026 Prospectus.*

## 10. PROGRAM SELECTION

### 10.1 Branch Allocation Mechanism
- Branch allocation is **100% merit-based** and determined strictly through CEE options entered by the candidate during the Centralized Allotment Process (CAP).
- An applicant cannot manually choose or switch branches at CET outside the CEE allotment system.

### 10.2 Branch Change Rules (Post-Admission)
- **Branch Change/Transfer:** Inter-branch transfers after joining are governed strictly by **KTU regulations** and **DTE Kerala guidelines**.
- **Conditions:** If permitted by KTU for a given academic batch, branch changes occur only at the end of the 1st Year (prior to 3rd semester), based on first-year academic performance (CGPA), availability of vacant seats in the target branch, and strictly within prescribed university quota limits.
- Candidates must not assume an automatic right to change branches after admission.

## 12. SPECIAL ADMISSION CATEGORIES

As a pure Government Engineering College, CET’s admission rules for special categories are strictly regulated:

- **Management Quota:** **NONE.** CET does NOT have any management quota or direct paid management seats.
- **NRI Quota:** **NONE.** CET does NOT offer self-financing NRI seats in standard B.Tech programs.
- **Lateral Entry Scheme (LET):**
  - **Target Candidates:** Diploma holders in Engineering/Technology or B.Sc graduates.
  - **Entry Point:** Direct admission into the 2nd Year (3rd Semester) of B.Tech.
  - **Authority:** Conducted via a separate State Lateral Entry Test / Entrance Rank List managed by DTE Kerala / CEE Kerala.
- **Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW):** Supernumerary seats filled exclusively through KEAM CAP based on merit and verified family income below the state threshold.

## 13. CANCELLATION / WITHDRAWAL

### 13.1 Admission Cancellation Procedure
If a candidate wishes to cancel their seat at CET to join another course or institution:

1. **Application:** Submit a formal written application signed by both the student and parent/guardian to the Principal, College of Engineering Trivandrum.
2. **Clearance (No Dues):** Obtain clearance certificates from all relevant departments (Department Library, Central Library, Laboratories, Hostels, PTA).
3. **CEE Rules Compliance:** If cancellation occurs during active CEE allotment rounds, the cancellation must be logged through the CEE portal as per the timeline set by CEE.
4. **Return of Certificates:** Upon processing the clearance, original certificates deposited during admission (e.g., 10+2 marksheet, pass certificate) will be returned.

### 13.2 Fee Refund & Penalty Rules
- **Refund Authority:** Refund of fees paid to CEE is handled strictly in accordance with CEE Kerala Prospectus rules and AICTE fee refund guidelines.
- **Timelines:**
  - Full/partial refund is granted if cancellation occurs prior to the CEE cut-off date.
  - **Late Cancellations / Liquidated Damages:** If a candidate cancels after the final CEE allotment cut-off date resulting in a seat remaining vacant, liquidated damages or fee forfeiture rules as declared in the KEAM 2026 Prospectus shall strictly apply.

    Your allotment will be automatically cancelled, and all options registered in that phase will be lost.

33. **Does CET charge additional capitation or donation fees?**
    No. CET is a pure Government Institution and does NOT charge any donation, capitation, or hidden fees.

### Special Categories & Rules
34. **Is there a Management Quota at CET?**
    No. CET does not have any management quota. 100% of seats are allotted based on merit and government rules via CEE.

35. **Does CET offer an NRI Quota for B.Tech?**
    No. There is no separate NRI quota for standard B.Tech admissions at CET.

36. **How does Lateral Entry admission work at CET?**
    Diploma holders and B.Sc graduates can apply for 2nd-year B.Tech admission via the State B.Tech Lateral Entry Entrance Examination.

37. **What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme?**
    TFW is an AICTE scheme offering full tuition fee waivers to high-merit students with annual family income below specified limits, allocated via CEE CAP.

### Post-Admission & Cancellation
38. **Can I change my B.Tech branch after joining CET?**
    Inter-branch transfer is governed strictly by KTU regulations at the end of Year 1 (Semester 2), subject to CGPA merit and available vacancies.

39. **How can I cancel my admission at CET?**
    Submit a formal application signed by student and parent to the Principal, obtain No Dues clearances, and follow CEE cancellation guidelines.

40. **Will I get a refund if I cancel my seat at CET?**
    Refunds are processed according to CEE Kerala and AICTE rules based on the date of cancellation relative to the admission cut-off date.

41. **Who do I contact if I face technical issues on the KEAM portal?**
    Contact the CEE Kerala helpline or official support email listed on `www.cee.kerala.gov.in`.

42. **Where can I view official B.Tech admission notices for CET?**
    On the official CEE portal (`www.cee.kerala.gov.in`) and the CET official website (`www.cet.ac.in`).
