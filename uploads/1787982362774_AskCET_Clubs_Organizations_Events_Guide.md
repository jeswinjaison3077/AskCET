# AskCET — Clubs, Organizations & Events Guide

## PART I — STUDENT CLUBS & ORGANIZATIONS

### 1. Clubs Overview
Student clubs and organizations at the College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET) are vibrant, student-driven communities that cater to a wide range of interests, from advanced robotics to classical arts. 

**What student clubs are:**
Clubs are official or semi-official student groups formed around specific technical, cultural, or social interests. They operate under the guidance of student coordinators and faculty advisors.

**Why students join clubs:**
Students join clubs to build a peer network, gain practical hands-on experience, develop leadership qualities, and pursue their passions outside the academic curriculum. 

**How clubs contribute to development:**
*   **Technical:** Provide platforms to build projects (e.g., RC planes, race cars) and participate in national hackathons.
*   **Cultural:** Offer avenues to showcase talents in dance, music, and literature at college and inter-college festivals.
*   **Professional:** Student chapters of global organizations (like IEEE, ASME) offer networking, industry exposure, and skill-building.
*   **Personal:** Enhance soft skills, teamwork, and time management through event organization and volunteering.

**Scope of clubs:**
Most clubs are open to all students across different branches and years. However, Department Associations (e.g., ATRADICS for CSE) are generally specific to the students of that department. Professional chapters may have membership criteria based on the parent organization.

**How students can discover active clubs:**
Students can discover clubs through the official CET Club Platform (clubs.cet.ac.in), orientation programs, official club social media pages, and recruitment drives held at the beginning of the academic year.

## PART II — JOINING CLUBS

### 8. How to Join a Club
Joining a club at CET typically involves the following steps:
1.  **Finding the Club:** Check the CET Clubs platform (clubs.cet.ac.in) or attend the first-year club orientation.
2.  **Recruitment Announcements:** Look out for official posters on campus notice boards or the club's official social media handles.
3.  **Registration/Application:** Fill out a Google form or register on the club's portal.
4.  **Selection Process:** 
    *   *Open to all:* Some clubs (like IEDC general membership or GDG) allow anyone to join.
    *   *Audition-based:* Cultural clubs (Dance, Music) require auditions.
    *   *Task/Interview-based:* Technical competitive teams (AeroCET, Herakles Racing) often assign a technical task followed by an interview.
5.  **Confirmation:** Selected students are added to official communication groups.

### 9. Club Recruitment
*   **Timing:** Major recruitment drives happen in the odd semester (August/September), shortly after first-year students arrive.
*   **Announcements:** Posters on campus, announcements in class groups, and WhatsApp/Instagram blasts.
*   **Process:** Varies heavily. A technical team might conduct a 2-day workshop followed by a selection test. A cultural team will hold an audition evening. If a club has no fixed schedule, they may recruit on a rolling basis or prior to a major competition.

### 10. Club Membership
*   **Eligibility:** Generally open to all enrolled students.
*   **Fees:** Professional chapters (like IEEE, ASCE, ASME) require an official membership fee dictated by the parent organization. Most internal cultural and technical clubs do not have membership fees (Not verified — refer to the latest official CET announcement for current internal fee policies).
*   **Expectations:** Members are expected to attend meetings, contribute to projects, and volunteer during events. Inactive members may be removed from competitive teams.

## PART VI — EVENT PARTICIPATION

### 20. How Students Can Participate
Students can engage in events in four distinct roles:
1.  **Participant:** Registers to compete in hackathons, cultural events, or sports.
2.  **Volunteer:** Assists in crowd control, hospitality, or registration on the day of the event.
3.  **Organizer / Coordinator:** Core committee members who plan sponsorships, logistics, and scheduling.
4.  **Club Member:** Represents a specific club putting up a stall or exhibition at the event.

### 21. Event Registration
*   **Find Announcement:** Check the official fest website (e.g., drishti.cet.ac.in).
*   **Register:** Use the official portal provided (do not use third-party unverified links).
*   **Payment:** Pay the registration or workshop fee via the official payment gateway.
*   **Confirmation:** Receive an entry QR code or ID card.

### 22. Volunteering
Massive events like Dhwani and Drishti require hundreds of student volunteers. Opportunities include:
*   Event Management & Stage Logistics
*   Registration Desk & Hospitality (Guest reception)
*   Technical Support (Audio/Visual, IT)
*   Media, Photography (usually via CET Shutterbugs), and Publicity.

## PART XI — GLOSSARY

*   **Student Club:** An interest-based group of students (e.g., Dance Club, AeroCET).
*   **Student Chapter:** A collegiate branch of a larger professional organization (e.g., IEEE Student Branch).
*   **Technical Club:** A group focused on engineering, coding, or technology (e.g., Herakles Racing).
*   **Cultural Club:** A group focused on arts, music, dance, or literature.
*   **Professional Chapter:** See Student Chapter. Focuses on career/industry networking.
*   **Recruitment:** The process of inducting new members into a club.
*   **Audition:** A performance-based selection process for cultural clubs.
*   **Volunteer:** A student helping with the logistics and execution of an event.
*   **Organizer:** Core committee member responsible for planning an event.
*   **Participant:** An individual or team competing or attending an event.
*   **Hackathon:** A time-bound competitive event to build software or hardware solutions.
*   **Workshop:** A short, intensive training session on a specific skill.
*   **Fest:** A large-scale festival (technical or cultural).
*   **Inter-college Event:** An event open to students from colleges other than CET.
*   **Event Edition:** The specific occurrence of an annual event in a given year (e.g., Drishti 2024).
