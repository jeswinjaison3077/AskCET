# PART I — HOSTEL

### 1. Hostel Overview
The College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET) provides residential facilities for students, subject to availability and eligibility criteria.
*   **Hostel Availability:** CET operates separate hostels for male and female students (Men's Hostels and Women's Hostels), as well as dedicated accommodation for postgraduate (PG) students.
*   **Purpose:** To provide safe, accessible, and convenient accommodation for outstation students pursuing their studies at CET.
*   **Student Eligibility:** B.Tech, B.Arch, M.Tech, MCA, and PhD students. Since capacity is limited, admission is not guaranteed for all students.
*   **General Hostel Structure:** Managed by the Hostel Warden (typically a senior faculty member), Resident Tutors (RT), and Matrons/Hostel Managers. 
*   **Residential Facilities:** Standard shared rooms for undergraduates, with single/double occupancy options usually reserved for senior/PG students (subject to availability).

### 2. Hostel Facilities
Depending on the specific hostel block, verifiable facilities include:
*   **Rooms:** Basic furniture including a cot, table, and chair per student.
*   **Study Areas:** Designated reading or common study rooms.
*   **Common Rooms:** Equipped with television and basic indoor recreation.
*   **Internet/Wi-Fi:** Access to the campus network (CETNET).
*   **Mess:** In-house dining facility managed either by a student committee (dividing system) or a fixed contract system.
*   **Drinking Water:** Purified drinking water systems installed in blocks.
*   **Electricity:** Regular power supply with generator backup for essential areas.
*   **Laundry:** Washing areas available; specialized laundry facilities TBA.
*   **Recreation Facilities:** Access to nearby sports grounds and indoor games.
*   **Security:** 24/7 security personnel at hostel gates.
*   **Maintenance Facilities:** Dedicated staff for cleaning common areas and bathrooms.

### 3. Hostel Eligibility & Allocation
*   **Who Can Apply:** Registered students of CET residing outside the Trivandrum city limits.
*   **Eligibility Requirements:** Based on the distance of the student's home residence from the college, academic merit (entrance rank/CGPA), and reservation categories (SC/ST).
*   **Application Procedure:** Online application through the official CET campus portal during the admission period or the start of the academic year.
*   **Allocation Process:** A rank list for hostel admission is published based on the priority criteria. 
*   **Priority Criteria:** Out-of-state students, SC/ST categories, differently-abled students, and students living in remote districts generally receive higher priority.
*   **Room Allocation:** Managed by the Hostel Warden/RTs. Freshers are usually grouped together.
*   **Renewal:** Hostel admission must be renewed annually. Renewal depends on academic progress and strict adherence to disciplinary rules.
*   **Vacating Hostel:** Students must submit a vacating form, clear all mess dues, and return room keys to receive their caution deposit.

### 5. Hostel Application Process
1.  **Check Eligibility:** Ensure distance and category criteria are met.
2.  **Obtain/Application Access:** Log in to the official CET student portal using the admission/student ID.
3.  **Submit Application:** Fill out the online hostel application form within the stipulated deadline.
4.  **Submit Documents:** Upload/provide address proof, allotment memo, and category certificate (if applicable).
5.  **Allocation:** Check the official hostel allotment list published by the CET Hostel Office.
6.  **Pay Applicable Fees:** Pay the caution deposit, admission fee, and mess advance via the designated online payment gateway (e.g., SB Collect).
7.  **Room Assignment:** Report to the Hostel Office with the fee receipt to receive the room allocation.
8.  **Check-in:** Move into the assigned room and register with the Resident Tutor/Matron.

### 6. Hostel Rules
*   **Entry and Exit:** Students must punch in/out or sign the register when leaving and entering the premises.
*   **Timings:** Strict curfew timings apply. (Exact timings: TBA / Refer to Latest Official Information).
*   **Visitors:** Visitors (including parents) are allowed only during designated visiting hours and in specific visitor areas. They are generally not allowed inside student rooms.
*   **Leave:** Prior permission from the Warden/Resident Tutor is required for overnight leave or going home.
*   **Overnight Absence:** Unauthorized overnight absence is a severe disciplinary offense.
*   **Room Changes:** Mutual room exchanges or changing rooms without the Warden's written permission is prohibited.
*   **Cleanliness:** Students are responsible for the cleanliness of their rooms.
*   **Noise:** Strict silence hours must be maintained during the night and exam periods.
*   **Prohibited Activities:** Ragging, consumption of alcohol, smoking, and use of illegal substances are strictly banned and will result in immediate expulsion and legal action.
*   **Electronic Appliances:** Heavy electrical appliances (heaters, irons, induction stoves) are strictly prohibited due to fire safety and power limits.
*   **Property Damage:** Cost of damages to hostel property will be recovered from the responsible student(s) or the entire block.
*   **Discipline:** Violation of rules will lead to disciplinary action, including expulsion from the hostel.

### 7. Mess
*   **Mess Arrangements:** Hostels operate messes to provide regular meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Tea/Snacks, Dinner).
*   **Meal Timings:** Specific timings for each meal. (TBA / Refer to Hostel Notice Board).
*   **Payment:** Generally operated on a dividing system where the total monthly expense is divided by the number of inmates, or a fixed monthly rate. 
*   **Food Arrangements:** Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian options are generally available.
*   **Mess Complaints:** Can be raised to the student Mess Secretary or the Warden.
*   **Leave/Mess Deductions:** Students on approved leave for a continuous specified number of days (usually 3 or more) may apply for a mess reduction, subject to prior written notice to the mess manager.

### 8. Hostel Maintenance & Complaints
*   **Electrical/Plumbing/Furniture:** Write the complaint in the Maintenance Register kept with the Matron/Security or submit it via the CET hostel portal.
*   **Internet Problems:** Report to the CETNET representative in the hostel or log a ticket with the campus IT center.
*   **Mess Issues:** Report directly to the student Mess Committee or Resident Tutor.
*   **Safety Concerns:** Immediately contact the Resident Tutor, Matron, or Campus Security.

# PART III — SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

### 17. Scholarship Overview
CET students can access various financial aids, provided they meet specific criteria:
*   **Central Government Scholarships:** Hosted on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), including Merit-cum-Means (MCM), Pragati (for female students), and Saksham (for specially-abled students).
*   **Kerala Government Scholarships:** Managed via the E-grantz portal for SC/ST/OEC/OBC and economically weaker sections (KPCR).
*   **Merit-based Scholarships:** Based on KEAM entrance rank or KTU university results.
*   **Need-based Financial Assistance:** For students with low family income.
*   **Institution-specific Assistance:** Offered by CET Alumni Association (CETAA) and the Parent Teacher Association (PTA).

### 19. Scholarship Eligibility
*   **Academic Requirements:** Most merit and central scholarships require a minimum percentage (usually 50% or 60%) in the previous qualifying examination or current CGPA without backlogs.
*   **Income Requirements:** Strict annual family income limits apply (e.g., under ₹2.5 Lakhs or ₹8 Lakhs, depending on the scheme). Must be backed by a valid Income Certificate from the Village Officer.
*   **Category Requirements:** Valid caste/community certificate required for SC/ST/OBC/Minority scholarships.
*   **Residency Requirements:** Kerala State scholarships require domicile/nativity certificates.
*   **Other Conditions:** A student can typically only receive *one* government scholarship at a time.

### 20. Scholarship Application Process
1.  **Identify Scholarship:** Determine eligibility for State (E-grantz), Central (NSP), or Institutional scholarships.
2.  **Prepare Documents:** Gather all mandated certificates (Income, Caste, Nativity, Bank details).
3.  **Register:** Create an account on the relevant portal (NSP / E-grantz).
4.  **Complete Application:** Fill in academic, personal, and bank account details accurately.
5.  **Upload Documents:** Scan and upload the required documents.
6.  **Submit:** Submit the application online before the deadline.
7.  **Verification:** Submit a printed copy of the application and original documents to the CET Academic/Scholarship Section for institutional verification.
8.  **Selection:** Authorities verify and select candidates.
9.  **Scholarship Disbursement:** Amounts are directly credited to the student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

### 22. Scholarship Renewal
*   **Renewal Eligibility:** Must maintain a minimum required CGPA (e.g., above 50% or passing all subjects) and good conduct.
*   **Income Limits:** Must continue to meet the income criteria.
*   **Renewal Procedure:** Apply through the "Renewal" tab on NSP or E-grantz. Do not register as a new user.
*   **Required Documents:** Previous year marklists and updated income certificate (if the previous one has expired).
*   **Deadlines:** Usually announced at the beginning of the academic year.
*   **Conditions:** Failing a semester or dropping out may lead to cancellation of the scholarship.

### 23. Fee Concessions & Waivers
*   **Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW):** A scheme under AICTE/KEAM where 5% of seats are given a complete tuition fee waiver based on merit and income. This is allotted during KEAM counseling, not after joining.
*   **SC/ST/OEC Concessions:** Eligible students get full tuition fee exemptions and allowances from the Government of Kerala.
*   *(Note: A fee waiver exempts you from paying the fee to the college. A scholarship usually involves paying the fee first, and receiving money in your bank account later, though E-grantz acts as a waiver for certain categories).*

# PART V — COMMON STUDENT SCENARIOS

**Hostel**
*   **I want to apply for hostel:** Log in to the CET campus portal during the admission window. Fill out the application, upload your allotment and address proof, and wait for the rank list.
*   **I have a hostel maintenance problem:** Write your complaint in the Maintenance Register kept with the Matron/Security, or inform the Resident Tutor.
*   **I need hostel leave:** Submit a written leave application (or via the hostel portal) to your Resident Tutor/Warden prior to leaving.
*   **I want to change rooms:** Room changes are not allowed without written permission. Submit a formal request citing valid reasons to the Hostel Warden.

**Library**
*   **I want to borrow a book:** Take the book to the circulation desk along with your Smart College ID Card.
*   **I lost a library book:** Inform the Librarian immediately to avoid accumulating late fines. You will be instructed to either buy a replacement copy of the exact same edition or pay the fine.
*   **I need access to an e-journal:** Connect to the campus Wi-Fi (CETNET) and visit the publisher's site, or use the library's remote access portal if off-campus.
*   **I have an overdue book:** Return it as soon as possible. You must pay the accrued daily fine at the circulation desk to restore your borrowing privileges.

**Scholarships**
*   **Which scholarships can I apply for?** Check E-grantz (if SC/ST/OEC/OBC from Kerala), NSP (for Central/MCM/Pragati), and CETAA notifications based on your income and category.
*   **What documents do I need?** You will primarily need an Income Certificate, Caste/Community Certificate, Nativity Certificate, Aadhaar, Bank Passbook, and previous marklists.
*   **How do I renew my scholarship?** Do not create a new account. Log in to the portal (NSP or E-grantz) where you originally applied, select "Renewal," and upload your latest marklists.
*   **My scholarship payment has not arrived:** Check your application status on the portal. Ensure your bank account is Aadhaar-seeded. Institutional delays can be checked with the CET Academic (Scholarship) Section.

**Student Services**
*   **I need a bonafide certificate:** Write an application addressed to the Principal, get it recommended by your Staff Advisor/HoD, and submit it to the Academic Section.
*   **I lost my ID card:** Report the loss, pay the ID card replacement fee at the Accounts section/online, and submit the receipt with a photo to the Academic Office.
*   **I have a fee problem:** Take your payment reference number/receipt and visit the Accounts Section for rectification.
*   **I need an academic document:** (Like TC or Migration) Complete the "No-Dues" clearance process from the library, hostel, and department, then apply at the Academic Office.
*   **I want to raise a grievance:** Approach your Staff Advisor first. If unresolved, submit a written complaint to the HoD, or use the official online Grievance Portal.
*   **I need placement information:** Register with the CGPU (Career Guidance and Placement Unit) during the specified window and check their official notice boards/emails.

31. **What scholarships are available?** E-grantz, NSP (MCM, Pragati), CETAA, and PTA scholarships.
32. **Who is eligible for E-grantz?** SC/ST/OEC/OBC and economically weaker students from Kerala.
33. **What documents are required?** Income certificate, caste certificate, Aadhaar, bank passbook, and marklists.
34. **How do I apply?** Online via NSP or E-grantz, followed by document submission to the college.
35. **How do I renew a scholarship?** Apply via the 'Renewal' section on the respective portal using current marklists.
36. **Are there fee concessions?** Yes, SC/ST/OEC students receive fee exemptions from the Govt of Kerala.
37. **What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW)?** An AICTE scheme granting full tuition waiver, allotted during KEAM.
38. **Where do I submit hardcopies of my application?** To the Academic Section (Scholarship desk) at CET.
39. **Can I hold two scholarships at once?** No, government rules prohibit holding multiple scholarships.
40. **How is the money paid?** Via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to your Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
41. **Who provides CETAA scholarships?** The CET Alumni Association.
42. **Do I need a new income certificate every year?** Only if your previous one has expired (usually valid for 1 year).
43. **Is there a scholarship for female students?** Yes, the Pragati scholarship under AICTE.
44. **What if my scholarship is delayed?** Check portal status or contact the CET Academic Section.
45. **Does back-paper (failure) affect my scholarship?** Yes, most scholarships require passing grades for renewal.

# PART VIII — GLOSSARY

*   **Hostel:** On-campus residential accommodation for students.
*   **Mess:** The dining facility inside the hostel that provides daily meals to inmates.
*   **Library Membership:** Automatic access granted to students allowing them to borrow books using their ID card.
*   **Digital Library:** The electronic collection of e-books, journals, and databases available to students.
*   **Scholarship:** Financial aid awarded to a student based on academic merit, category, or financial need, usually credited to their bank account.
*   **Fee Waiver:** A total or partial exemption from paying college tuition fees (e.g., TFW).
*   **Fee Concession:** A reduction in fees given to specific categories (e.g., SC/ST/OEC).
*   **Bonafide Certificate:** An official document issued by the college proving that the student is genuinely studying at the institution.
*   **Grievance:** A formal complaint raised by a student regarding academic, administrative, or personal issues on campus.
*   **Placement:** The process facilitated by CGPU where companies recruit students directly from the campus.
*   **Internship:** A temporary work experience in an industry/company, often required for academic credits.
*   **Academic Office:** The central administrative section handling admissions, student records, fees, and certificates.
*   **Examination Office/Cell:** The department managing all logistics for internal and KTU university exams.
*   **Student Welfare:** Services and committees ensuring the mental health, safety, and well-being of students (e.g., Anti-ragging, Women's cell).
