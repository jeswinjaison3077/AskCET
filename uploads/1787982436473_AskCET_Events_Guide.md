# AskCET — Complete Events, Festivals & Activities Guide

## PART I — EVENTS OVERVIEW & FRAMEWORK

### 1. Events Ecosystem at CET
The College of Engineering Trivandrum (CET) hosts a diverse, highly structured calendar of events ranging from national-level techno-cultural festivals to departmental symposia, sports tournaments, and professional seminars. 

**Classification of Events:**
*   **Technical Fests:** Large-scale national symposiums focusing on engineering, coding, robotics, and innovation (e.g., *Drishti*, *TechRave*).
*   **Cultural Fests:** Flagship arts and entertainment festivals drawing participants from across South India (e.g., *Dhwani*).
*   **Department Events:** Specialized technical paper presentations, workshops, and quizzes organized by individual department associations.
*   **Sports & Athletics:** Inter-collegiate athletic meets and KTU zonal tournaments managed by the Department of Physical Education and student sports bodies.
*   **Professional & Academic Seminars:** Guest lectures, industry interaction sessions, and alumni meets organized by professional chapters (IEEE, ISTE, ASME, ASCE).

**Recurring vs. One-Time/Historical Events:**
*   *Recurring Annual Events:* Drishti, Dhwani, TechRave, Department Fests (ATRADICS, TRIODCE, METAMES).
*   *Historical/One-Time Events:* Specific historical editions (e.g., Drishti 2024, Dhwani 2025) serve as reference points for past scales of operation; current year editions must be verified via official announcements.

## PART II — MAJOR FESTIVALS & FLAGSHIP EVENTS

### 2. Dhwani (Flagship Cultural Festival)
*   **Event Name:** Dhwani
*   **Category:** Cultural Festival / Arts / Entertainment
*   **Organizer:** CET Student Union & Cultural Committees
*   **Frequency:** Annual (Typically held in the even semester)
*   **Target Participants:** Open to engineering and arts students across India (Inter-collegiate)
*   **Main Activities:**
    *   *Pro-Shows & CET Nights:* Massive musical concerts, DJ nights, and celebrity band performances.
    *   *Competitions:* Rangam (Short film competition), classical/Western dance battles, fashion shows, street plays (Choreo), and musical band showdowns.
    *   *Literary & Arts:* Debates, creative writing, quiz competitions, painting, and sculpture exhibitions.
*   **Venue:** CET Main Campus (Open-air theatre, college auditorium, department grounds)
*   **Registration & Ticketing:** Registration via official fest portal (`dhwani.cet.ac.in`). Pro-shows require official entry wristbands/passes purchased through authorized campus counters.
*   **Official Source:** `cet.ac.in` / official Dhwani portal.

### 3. Drishti (National Technical Festival)
*   **Event Name:** Drishti
*   **Category:** National Technical Festival
*   **Organizer:** CET Tech Clubs & Student Body
*   **Frequency:** Annual (Typically held in the odd/even academic window)
*   **Target Participants:** Engineering students, tech enthusiasts, innovators, and developers nationwide.
*   **Main Activities:**
    *   *Hackathons:* *BeyondHack* and specialized coding sprints.
    *   *Competitions:* Robotics challenges, line-following, RC plane design contests, CAD modeling, and bridge-building competitions.
    *   *Workshops:* Hands-on technical sessions on AI/ML, IoT, Cyber Security, Robotics, and Advanced Manufacturing.
    *   *Project Exhibitions:* Showcasing student innovations, startup prototypes, and industrial products.
*   **Venue:** CET Campus (Departments, Computer Labs, Main Auditorium)
*   **Registration:** Online registration via `drishti.cet.ac.in`. Nominal workshop/event fees apply depending on the specific module.
*   **Official Source:** `drishti.cet.ac.in`

### 4. TechRave (SAEINDIA CET Technical Fest)
*   **Event Name:** TechRave
*   **Category:** Automotive & Mechanical Technical Fest
*   **Organizer:** SAEINDIA CET Collegiate Club
*   **Frequency:** Annual
*   **Target Participants:** Mechanical, Automotive, and general engineering students.
*   **Main Activities:** Automobile exhibitions, vehicle dynamics workshops, off-roading vehicle showcases (featuring Herakles Racing ATVs), and mechanical design challenges.
*   **Venue:** CET Mechanical Department & Grounds
*   **Official Source:** `clubs.cet.ac.in` / CET official portal.

## PART IV — SPORTS & RECREATIONAL EVENTS

### 6. Sports Events & Tournaments
CET emphasizes physical education and competitive sports through internal meets and inter-collegiate tournaments.
*   **Inter-Department Sports Meets:** Annual competitions where departments (CSE, ME, CE, ECE, EEE, Arch) compete in athletics, cricket, football, basketball, and volleyball for the inter-department championship trophy.
*   **KTU Zonal Tournaments:** APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University frequently designates CET as the host or participant for zonal tournaments in Basketball, Badminton, Table Tennis, Football, and Cricket.
*   **Staff vs. Student Matches:** Traditional friendly fixtures held during special college commemorative days.
*   **Registration & Trials:** Handled by the Department of Physical Education and student sports coordinators at the start of the academic year.

## PART V — EVENT PARTICIPATION & ROLES

### 7. Understanding Student Roles in Events
Students can engage in campus events through four distinct capacities:
1.  **Participant:** Registers to compete in hackathons, cultural events, sports matches, or paper presentations. Eligible for cash prizes, merit certificates, and KTU activity points.
2.  **Volunteer:** Assists the core committee with crowd management, stage setup, hospitality, registration desks, and participant guidance. Ideal for gaining event-management experience.
3.  **Organizer / Coordinator:** Senior student leaders and core committee members who manage corporate sponsorships, publicity, budgeting, scheduling, and celebrity coordination.
4.  **Club Exhibitor:** Members of technical or cultural clubs who set up interactive display booths, stalls, or demonstrations during Drishti or Dhwani.

### 8. Registration Guidelines
*   **Official Announcements:** Always verify events through official channels (`cet.ac.in`, `drishti.cet.ac.in`, `dhwani.cet.ac.in`, or official verified social media pages).
*   **Eligibility Check:** Review whether the event is open exclusively to CET students or open to external/inter-college participants.
*   **Portal Registration:** Fill out the digital application form on the designated portal. Do not use unverified third-party registration links.
*   **Fee Payment:** Complete secure digital payments for workshop/event fees if applicable. Keep payment transaction receipts.
*   **Confirmation:** Obtain the digital entry pass, QR code, or confirmation email required for physical gate/venue check-in.

## PART VI — EVENT RULES, CONDUCT & SAFETY

### 9. General Guidelines & Protocols
*   **Identification:** Valid CET Identity Cards are mandatory for all on-campus students attending or volunteering at any fest. External participants must carry their native institution ID cards along with event passes.
*   **Access Control:** Wristbands and digital passes are strictly enforced for premium events and pro-shows during Dhwani. Transferring passes is prohibited.
*   **Code of Conduct:** Strict anti-ragging, anti-harassment, and behavioral policies apply. Misbehavior results in immediate disciplinary action by the college authorities.
*   **Prohibited Items:** Alcohol, illegal substances, firecrackers, and sharp/dangerous objects are strictly banned across all campus venues.
*   **Safety & Security:** Security personnel, faculty coordinators, and student volunteers operate at all entry/exit points and high-density zones to ensure crowd safety.

## PART VIII — EVENT FAQs (FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS)

1. **What are the major events conducted at CET?**
   The two flagship events are *Dhwani* (cultural festival) and *Drishti* (technical festival).
2. **When is Dhwani conducted?**
   Dhwani is typically conducted annually during the even semester. Exact dates vary each year and are announced via official channels.
3. **When is Drishti conducted?**
   Drishti is CET's annual national technical festival, usually scheduled during the academic year.
4. **Are non-CET students allowed to participate in CET fests?**
   Yes, Dhwani and Drishti are major inter-college, national-level events open to students from other engineering and arts colleges.
5. **How do I register for Drishti events or workshops?**
   Registrations are conducted online through the official portal at `drishti.cet.ac.in`.
6. **How do I get tickets for Dhwani pro-shows?**
   Pro-show passes and entry wristbands can be purchased through official campus ticket counters or the designated fest website.
7. **Can first-year students volunteer for college fests?**
   Yes, first-year students are encouraged to apply as volunteers during recruitment drives held by organizing committees prior to the fests.
8. **Are event registrations free?**
   Some exhibitions and minor competitions are free, but specialized workshops, hackathons, and pro-shows typically require a registration fee or ticket pass.
9. **Where are official event announcements published?**
   Announcements are published on the official CET website (`cet.ac.in`), fest domains, and verified social media handles.
10. **Do I need my college ID card to attend events?**
    Yes, a valid CET ID card is mandatory for internal students. External participants must carry their college ID and event pass.
11. **What is BeyondHack?**
    BeyondHack is a flagship competitive hackathon conducted as part of the Drishti technical festival.
12. **What is Rangam?**
    Rangam is a premier short film and filmmaking competition held during the Dhwani cultural festival.
13. **Are attendance/duty leaves provided for event participation?**
    Duty leave is generally sanctioned for students officially representing CET in external inter-college competitions, subject to university and college guidelines.
14. **Who organizes department-specific symposiums?**
    Department associations such as ATRADICS (CSE), TRIODCE (Civil), METAMES (Mech), and ARC (ECE).
15. **What sports events are held at CET?**
    Inter-departmental sports meets and KTU zonal tournaments for basketball, football, cricket, volleyball, and athletics.
16. **Is accommodation provided for inter-college participants?**
    For major fests like Dhwani and Drishti, limited accommodation assistance may be provided subject to official registration terms.
17. **What is TechRave?**
    TechRave is an automotive-focused technical event organized by SAEINDIA CET.
18. **Can alumni attend college fests?**
    Yes, alumni are welcome and often participate in special alumni meets and networking sessions during fests.
19. **What happens if an event is postponed or rescheduled?**
    Official notifications are immediately posted on the main CET website and official event social media channels.
20. **Whom should I contact for event-related queries?**
    Contact the respective faculty advisors or student coordinators listed in the official event brochures.
21. **Are food stalls available during fests?**
    Yes, extensive food courts and authorized refreshment stalls are set up across campus during Dhwani and Drishti.
22. **What security measures are in place during CET Nights / Pro-shows?**
    Campus security, local law enforcement coordination, and student volunteer teams ensure strict entry screening and crowd control.
23. **Do participants receive certificates?**
    Yes, verified participants and winners of technical competitions, hackathons, and cultural events receive official certificates.
24. **Can I register on-spot for events?**
    While some events permit on-spot registration, online pre-registration is strongly recommended due to capped capacities.
25. **Are there cash prizes for competitions?**
    Yes, major events at Dhwani and Drishti feature substantial cash prize pools for winners.
26. **What is the dress code for attending technical symposiums?**
    Smart casuals or formal attire is recommended for academic and technical paper presentation events.
27. **How can student clubs participate in fests?**
    Clubs can set up exhibition zones, technical demonstration booths, or organize sub-events under the fest umbrella.
28. **Are there literary events during fests?**
    Yes, debate, quizzing, creative writing, and elocution competitions are hosted by literary societies during Dhwani and Drishti.
29. **What is the role of faculty coordinators during events?**
    Faculty members oversee administrative approvals, budgeting, safety compliance, and overall institutional guidance.
30. **Where can I view past photo/video highlights of CET fests?**
    Official highlights are hosted on the respective YouTube and Instagram handles of Dhwani, Drishti, and CET Shutterbugs.

## PART X — GLOSSARY OF EVENT TERMS

*   **Flagship Fest:** The primary, largest-scale annual festival hosted by the institution (e.g., Dhwani or Drishti).
*   **Pro-Show:** Professional celebrity musical concerts or DJ nights held during cultural festivals.
*   **Hackathon:** An intensive, time-bound coding or hardware building competition.
*   **Symposium:** A formal academic or technical conference featuring paper presentations and expert lectures.
*   **Zonal Tournament:** Inter-collegiate sports competitions grouped by geographical university zones (KTU zones).
*   **Core Committee:** The primary student organizing body responsible for planning and executing an event.
*   **Inter-collegiate:** Events open to participants from colleges other than CET.
*   **Event Edition:** The specific annual iteration of a recurring event (e.g., 2026 Edition).
