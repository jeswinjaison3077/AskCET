## 2. Program Structure

- **Number of Semesters:** 8, organized as 4 academic years (2 semesters each).
- **Semester Structure:** Semesters 1–2 cover common foundational Basic Science and Engineering Science courses. Semesters 3–5 introduce CSE Professional Core courses. Semesters 5–8 add Professional Electives, Open Electives, and project/seminar components with increasing weight toward independent work in the final year.
- **Course Categories used in this demo curriculum:**
  - **BSC** — Basic Science Course
  - **ESC** — Engineering Science Course
  - **HSMC** — Humanities and Social Science / Management Course
  - **MC** — Mandatory Course (non-credit)
  - **PCC** — Professional Core Course
  - **PEC** — Professional Elective Course
  - **OEC** — Open Elective Course
  - **PROJ** — Project / Seminar
- **Core Courses:** Delivered as PCC-category courses from Semester 3 onward.
- **Electives:** Professional Electives (PEC) from Semester 5 onward; Open Electives (OEC) from Semester 6 onward.
- **Laboratories:** Standalone lab courses and combined theory-lab (L-T-P with a practical component) courses throughout all 8 semesters.
- **Project:** Mini Project in Semester 6; Project Phase I in Semester 7; Project Phase II (or Internship) in Semester 8.
- **Internship:** Offered as an alternative track within Project Phase II (Semester 8) in this demo curriculum.
- **Seminar:** Semester 7.
- **Other Components:** Two non-credit Mandatory Courses (MC) in Semesters 2 and 3.

## 4. Course Categories

- **Basic Science Course (BSC):** Foundational mathematics, physics, and chemistry courses (Semesters 1–4) that build the scientific base for engineering study.
- **Engineering Science Course (ESC):** Cross-disciplinary engineering fundamentals (electrical/electronics basics, civil/mechanical basics, graphics, workshop, introductory programming) taken mainly in Semesters 1–2.
- **Humanities and Social Science / Management Course (HSMC):** Communication, economics, ethics, and management-oriented courses distributed across several semesters.
- **Mandatory Course (MC):** Non-credit courses required for degree completion but not counted toward the credit total (Environmental Science, Constitution of India in this demo curriculum).
- **Professional Core Course (PCC):** Core CSE subjects — data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, networks, and similar — forming the backbone of the program from Semester 2/3 onward.
- **Professional Elective (PEC):** CSE-specialization electives chosen by students from defined slots in Semesters 5–8.
- **Open Elective (OEC):** Electives students may choose from a wider set of offerings (including from other departments), taken in Semesters 6–8.
- **Project/Seminar (PROJ):** Mini Project, Seminar, Project Phase I, and Project Phase II/Internship components in Semesters 6–8.

## 7. Project / Internship

- **Mini Project (CS353):** Semester 6, 2 credits, a socially relevant, group-based project component. No prerequisite specified in this demo curriculum.
- **Seminar (CS403):** Semester 7, 2 credits. Involves preparing and presenting a technical seminar on a topic relevant to the CSE field.
- **Project Phase I (CS404):** Semester 7, 4 credits. Covers project topic identification, literature review, and initial design/implementation work.
- **Project Phase II / Internship (CS452):** Semester 8, 8 credits, prerequisite CS404. Students continue and complete the Project Phase I work, **or**, in this demo curriculum, may substitute an approved internship in place of Project Phase II.
- **Evaluation Components:** Not verified against an official source in this demo document. **Refer to the Academic Regulations document for detailed evaluation and assessment weightage rules for project and internship components.**
