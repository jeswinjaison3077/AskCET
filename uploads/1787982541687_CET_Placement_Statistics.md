## Department-Wise Overview & Major Recruiters

### 1. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
* **Packages:** High share of top-tier offers, reaching up to the campus maximum of **INR 33–34 LPA**. 
* **Top Roles:** Software Development Engineer (SDE), Systems Analyst, Data Engineer.
* **Major Recruiters:** Amazon, Oracle, Adobe, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Accenture.

### 2. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
* **Packages:** Strong core and software packages, averaging around **INR 7–8 LPA**.
* **Top Roles:** Embedded Engineer, VLSI Design Engineer, Software Developer.
* **Major Recruiters:** Texas Instruments, Bosch, Intuit, Oracle.

### 3. Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)
* **Packages:** Competitive core and cross-functional IT offers.
* **Top Roles:** Electrical Engineer, Power Systems Analyst, Software Engineer.
* **Major Recruiters:** ABB, Bosch, L&T, TCS.

### 4. Mechanical Engineering (ME)
* **Packages:** Solid placement in manufacturing, automotive, and core engineering firms.
* **Top Roles:** Design Engineer, Production Engineer, Operations Trainee.
* **Major Recruiters:** Mahindra, L&T, Ford, Nissan, Saint Gobain.

### 5. Civil Engineering (CE)
* **Packages:** Stable placements in major construction and real estate sectors.
* **Top Roles:** Site Engineer, Structural Designer, Project Coordinator.
* **Major Recruiters:** L&T Construction, Sobha Developers.
