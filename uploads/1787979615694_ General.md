## 1. College Overview

- **Full Name:** College of Engineering Trivandrum
- **Common Name / Abbreviation:** CET (also referred to as College of Engineering Thiruvananthapuram, CET Trivandrum)
- **Location:** Sreekaryam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India – 695016
- **Established:** 1939 (first classes started 3 July 1939), making it the first engineering college in the former Travancore State and the oldest technical institution in Kerala
- **Type:** Government engineering college, owned by the Government of Kerala and administered by the Department of Technical Education
- **University Affiliation:** APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), since 2015. Previously affiliated to the University of Kerala (1957–2015) and the University of Travancore (1939–1957)
- **Accreditation:** All B.Tech programs are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA) — CET's official website states this accreditation is valid till 31-12-2028
- **Campus:** The college moved to its present campus in 1960. Sources give campus size as approximately **80 acres** (CET's own official "About" page) — some third-party sources cite a larger figure (up to ~125 acres); the 80-acre figure from the official site is used as the primary reference here
- **Academic Focus:** Multidisciplinary technical education spanning engineering, architecture, computer applications, and management, with undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs
- **Motto:** *Karma jyāyo hyakarmaṇaḥ* — "Action is superior to inaction" (Bhagavad Gita 3:8)
- **Approximate Scale:** Around 5,000 students, supported by roughly 300 faculty members and 300 technical/ministerial staff (per CET's official site)

**Source:** CET Official Website (cet.ac.in), Wikipedia, CET Alumni Network

## 13. Examination System — General Overview

CET's B.Tech and other programs follow the examination framework of its affiliating university, **KTU (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University)**, since 2015 (for programs previously under University of Kerala regulations, KTU rules apply from the 2015 affiliation onward).

General structure (typical of KTU-affiliated colleges; CET-specific numeric requirements are not stated here as they were not verified):

- **Internal Assessment:** Continuous evaluation conducted at the college level (assignments, tests, attendance-linked components), per KTU regulations.
- **End-Semester Examinations:** Conducted under KTU's semester examination framework.
- **Semester System:** Programs are organized into 8 semesters for B.Tech (4-year duration) and appropriate semester structures for other programs.
- **Grades and Credits:** Assigned per the KTU credit and grading system.
- **Results:** Published through official KTU examination channels.
- **Supplementary Examinations / Revaluation:** Available per KTU examination regulations; specific processes (fees, deadlines, application methods) should be verified from current KTU/CET examination notifications.

**Distinction:** Day-to-day internal assessment administration is handled at the CET college level, while end-semester examinations, results publication, and revaluation/supplementary processes are governed by **KTU regulations** — students should check whether a given examination query is a CET-level or KTU-level matter.

**Sample/Demo Note:** Specific numeric requirements (minimum attendance %, pass marks, credit totals) are intentionally omitted here as unverified; a separate, verified **Examination Rules** document should hold these figures.
